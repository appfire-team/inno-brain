Strategy work doesn't compound. Opinions, not options. Decks, not decisions.

Drop in any document. InnoBrain extracts a knowledge graph, lets your team shape it with lived experience, and turns it into typed strategic artifacts via Playbooks — briefs, build-vs-buy decisions, PRDs, launch plans, opportunity scans.

Every artifact is citable, refinable, comparable across time. Every LLM call is framed by your rubric and remembers what you've already established.
